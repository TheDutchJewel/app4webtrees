# webtrees in the browser

wtWin is a window to webtrees, not a replacement. Administration of the tree stays on the webtrees site, and the program takes you there with one click.

## The webtrees menu

The entries open the matching webtrees page in the browser; sign in there if asked.

- **Places** and **Sources:** the lists of the tree.
- **Merge records**, **Find duplicates**, **Check tree**, **Data fixes:** the administration tools (tree managers only).
- **Export GEDCOM** and **Import GEDCOM** (managers only).

## A person in the browser

**File › Open webtrees in browser** opens the current person (or the home page); **Person › Open in webtrees** and the right-click menu do the same. On the person sheet, **Edit in webtrees** leads straight to the person page.

## What stays in webtrees

- Users, rights, privacy settings and approvals
- Modules, site design, site language
- Changing names, creating and linking sources and media objects, editing notes
- Server backups

## Changes made in the program

What you change in wtWin is in webtrees immediately, under your account and its rights. If the tree is set to "accept changes automatically", they apply at once; otherwise they wait as pending changes for a moderator. Moderators see pending changes in the **Home** section and accept or reject them there. **F5** reloads the tree after you changed something in the browser.
