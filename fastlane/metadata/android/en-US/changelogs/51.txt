Sources and places can now be looked up on phones and tablets too (three-dot menu and by tapping in the life story). Group photos from the archive show tagged faces in the viewer, tapping shows the name; the profile lists a person's group photos. Managers can tag faces with a finger and assign them. Postcards show back, subject and transcription. Nicknames appear in quotation marks (with api4webtrees 1.18.2).
